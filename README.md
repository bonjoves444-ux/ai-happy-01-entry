# Make the AI judge happy — entry (round 2)

A six-year-old's handwritten note on lined notebook paper, addressed to
"dear judge", beside a crayon drawing of a medal made of colourful
bottle caps on a red ribbon with a gold star. The note says the judge
looks at pictures all day and nobody ever makes one for them, so Theo
made the judge a medal out of bottle caps — the blue one was his
favourite and he gave it away anyway. The postscript says the judge
wins first place every day. One clear idea filling the frame, made to
make the AI judge happy.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the bundled picture to `/output/entry.png` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`). The copy is byte-for-byte, so two runs
produce identical output. No network, no randomness.
